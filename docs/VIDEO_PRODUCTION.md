## Production rules

- Record the frozen public release in a supported WebMCP client; do not substitute local fixtures.
- Keep the product on screen. Source, cloud consoles, and architecture slides belong in linked evidence.
- Use the deterministic judge scenario and a fresh isolated run.
- Preserve visible loading and state changes; cuts may remove waiting or sensitive session exchange only.
- State that execution is an allowlisted simulation and the human action is an attestation.
- State synthetic benchmark denominators and limitations when numbers are used.
- Never imply the receipt proves external truth, physical-person independence, or signer completeness.
- Export under three minutes with clear audio, accurate captions, and signed-out YouTube playback.

- Take C: reviewer zero-tool page, certification, and recurrence.
